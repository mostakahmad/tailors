How it works

A client journey you can actually click through.

The interface is real. The tape measurements are a clearly labeled demonstration, ready for a future measurement service to replace.

01

Set a body profile

Add an optional name, height, display units, and an illustrative body preset.

02

Capture two views

Upload, drop, or use the device camera for a front photo and a side photo. Or load sample illustrations.

03

Preview landmarks

When the on-device pose model loads, joints are drawn on the photographs. Samples skip this step.

04

Review the studio

Inspect illustrative measurements, the 3D mannequin, and a report you can edit, export, or print.

This browser actually does

  • Camera capture, upload, and local preview
  • Image type, size, and decode checks
  • On-device pose landmarks when the model loads
  • Unit conversion and manual edits
  • CSV, JSON, and browser print

This demo simulates

  • Chest, waist, hip, and other tailoring values
  • Body-shape presets
  • The 3D mannequin’s proportions
  • The sample measurement report

Ready for a real measurement service

The demo engine follows a MeasurementProvider contract. A validated service can replace it later without rewriting the studio, 3D view, or report.

    Concept Prototype — Measurements are illustrative and not suitable for tailoring.

    Photos are processed in this browser only. They are not uploaded, not saved in site storage, and not included in exports. A public pose model may download from a CDN; images are not sent with that request.

    Do not use these values for garment cutting, sizing decisions, or medical purposes. Photographs are not tape measurements.

    Body measurement experience · concept prototype

    Camera capture

    First visit

    Start with the sample body

    No photo is required for a first look. This path stays on this device, and the measurements stay a labeled demo.

    1. Load the sample body. Front and side illustrations fill the studio.
    2. Press Generate Measurement Preview.
    3. Review the report and the 3D model. The values are not tape measurements.

    Reset this session?

    Previews, edits, and the illustrative report will clear. Photos were never uploaded. This cannot be undone in the page.
